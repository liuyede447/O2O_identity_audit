# Licence decision required

No licence has been selected automatically because licensing is an author and
institutional decision.

Without a licence, default copyright law applies: users may view the public
repository, but reuse, modification, and redistribution are not broadly
granted. Before public release, the authors should choose a licence they are
authorised to apply to the original scripts and derived tables.

Common options to discuss with all authors are:

- MIT or BSD-3-Clause for permissive software reuse;
- Apache-2.0 when an explicit patent grant is desired;
- a separate CC-BY-4.0 or CC0 choice for derived tabular data, if permitted.

No licence selected here overrides the licences or terms of AI-TOD-v2,
VisDrone, PyTorch, Ultralytics, or other third-party dependencies.
