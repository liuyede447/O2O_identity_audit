# Figure captions for author approval

These captions formed the approval baseline for the figure set. Their panel structure and interpretation guards were synchronised into the authoritative main manuscript and Supplement on 2026-09-05, and final all-author approval was subsequently confirmed for v3.0.0.

## Figure 1. Read-only assignment-state measurement

**(a)** Controlled focal-ground-truth (GT) replay on a frozen AI-TOD-v2 image. The displayed rectangles are true annotations for GTs 17, 28, and 40; GT 28 is focal. The magnified inset overlays the solid green base GT and its dashed red one-input-pixel upward shift. Source pixels are enlarged by nearest-neighbour replication without image enhancement. Only the focal-GT centre is displaced, whereas model outputs, box extent, and non-focal GTs remain fixed. The fixed contract uses $\delta=1$ input pixel and the same absolute displacement for tiny and small objects; equivalent-area-side replay uses $\delta=\kappa s_g$ with $s_g=\sqrt{w_gh_g}$ and therefore scales the displacement with object size. **(b)** The same frozen candidate state feeds the native one-to-one (O2O) and one-to-many (O2M) assignment paths. The O2O path terminates in one loss-active identity, whereas the O2M path terminates in an assigned-positive set and its Top-1 rank readout; this Top-1 is not a uniquely supervised positive. **(c)** Base-to-shift comparison defines stable, identity-change, and undefined states. **(d)** The first observed boundary is read along one cardinal ray; the audit does not estimate a full two-dimensional minimum radius. OpenAI Codex (OpenAI; accessed August--September 2026) provided advisory input on the schematic layout; the authors produced and verified the final artwork and all scientific content.

## Figure 2. Stress-contract-dependent scale response

**(a)** O2O, O2M, and paired 8–16 minus 16–32 px contrasts under fixed one-pixel and equivalent-area-side stress. Points denote estimates and bars denote 95% stratified image-cluster bootstrap intervals. **(b)** Paired O2O-minus-O2M contrast at the three prespecified equivalent-area-side κ values in AI-TOD-v2 and VisDrone. The tested κ values occupy their true positions on a linear numerical axis; no connecting curve or continuous trend model is fitted. **(c)** Paired contrasts at six equally spaced categorical archived checkpoints labelled 0, 20, 40, 60, 80, and 300 (final). The archived `last.pt` state at 300 is distinct from the retained trainer-selected best checkpoint; no checkpoints were archived for epochs 100–280. **(d)** Compatible native-contract replication with YOLO26s and YOLOv10-S. Different native assignment semantics preclude a same-mechanism or broad architecture-generalisation claim.

## Figure 3. Eligibility and native-path localisation

**(a)** Native versus eligibility-locked O2O scale contrasts on the eligibility-lock support. This support differs from the primary endpoint support. **(b)** Exclusive first-observed pathway composition (left) and non-exclusive any-stage occurrence for eligibility, within-set rank, Top-k, conflict, and eligibility–rank co-occurrence (right). **(c)** Pre-resolution same-branch control. Filled markers denote fixed one-pixel stress and open markers equivalent-area-side stress. The eligibility-stable observational subset and secondary active-missing and geometry-clipping rows are reserved for supplementary placement. First-observed localisation depends on execution order; all pathway summaries are descriptive and are not causal contribution fractions or mediation effects.

## Figure 4. Grid-resolved cardinal boundary geometry

**(a)** Kaplan–Meier native-state persistence for the O2O loss-active identity and O2M assigned-set Top-1 rank state in the 8–16 and 16–32 px groups. **(b)** Aalen–Johansen cause-specific cumulative incidence for the first O2O boundary. **(c)** Absolute branch-specific restricted mean cardinal boundary distance (RMCBD) and the paired O2O-minus-O2M contrast at the primary truncation horizon τ=0.125. **(d)** Paired contrast across the six prespecified truncation horizons on a linear numerical τ axis. Lines connect tested horizons only; branch ordering persists, whereas magnitude increases with τ by construction. Base-undefined trajectories are excluded, right censoring is retained, and all distances refer to equivalent-area-side cardinal rays rather than a full two-dimensional minimum boundary.

## Figure 5. O2M Rank–Set decomposition

**(a)** Schematic distinction among rank-only turnover, simultaneous rank-and-set turnover, and set-only turnover with stable assigned-set Top-1. Filled circles denote the assigned-set Top-1; open circles denote other positives. **(b)** Native O2M Rank–Set contrasts under fixed and equivalent-area-side stress. **(c)** Weighted native positive-count distributions for the two scale groups, displayed as discrete lollipops. K=0 remains visible; shading marks only the K=3–7 overlap used for standardisation. **(d)** Full-support crude and K=3–7 count-standardised scale contrasts. Panels b and d use 16–32 minus 8–16 px throughout. The two estimates have different supports, and the standardisation is descriptive rather than a causal positive-count intervention.

## Figure 6. Margin and observed rank-crossing geometry

**(a)** Native active-relative margin versus observed fixed-pair rank-crossing distance among the 1,593 trajectories with an observed crossing (conditional Spearman ρ=0.628). The overlaid line joins ten margin-decile medians as a descriptive visual summary; no regression curve is fitted. **(b)** Weighted composition of observed crossing (5.4%), competing censoring, right censoring, and undefined states among all directional trajectories. **(c)** Conditional Spearman associations with observed rank crossing and eligibility-boundary distance on their separate observed supports. **(d)** Held-out diagnostic change after adding margin in AI-TOD-v2 and VisDrone; log-loss and Brier signs are reversed so that positive values consistently indicate improvement. These analyses concern conditional association and diagnostic increment, not a guaranteed predictor or intervention effect.

## Supplementary Figure S1. Audited primary-checkpoint assignment-state examples

Real-image stable, fragile, and post-shift undefined examples under the audited primary checkpoint. Each row shows a wider context, the base state, and the one-pixel shift state. Green solid boxes mark the focal GT, red dashed boxes the shifted GT, blue solid boxes the active candidate, and purple dashed boxes the runner candidate. Each detail inset reproduces every source pixel as a same-colour vector cell; no interpolation or image enhancement is applied. Image pixels are unchanged and only the focal-GT centre moves. The overlays are audited assignment states, not final detections. OpenAI Codex (OpenAI; accessed August--September 2026) provided advisory input on layout; all pixels and overlays were rendered deterministically from frozen author-provided assets without AI generation or alteration and were verified by the authors.
