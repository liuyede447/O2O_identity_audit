# Licence scope and publication decision

## Approved software and derived-data licence

The repository carries **GNU AGPL-3.0-only** because the audit executes against and redistributes a modified Ultralytics source snapshot whose files declare AGPL-3.0. The full licence text is in `LICENSE`.

All authors and the relevant institutional rights holder approved licensing the original scripts, modified source snapshot, and released derived tabular data under AGPL-3.0-only for v3.1.0.

## Excluded rights

The repository licence does not grant rights in AI-TOD-v2 or VisDrone images/annotations. Those datasets are not redistributed. The four author-trained checkpoints were approved for public distribution as separate v3.1.0 release assets under AGPL-3.0-only.

Derived tabular results and the complete 1/1024 dense-grid curve are distributed under the same repository licence.
