Data and code availability

Code, derived data, figure-source tables, the complete 1/1024 dense-grid records, and four protocol-matched checkpoints corresponding to this article are archived in GitHub release v3.2.0 (https://github.com/liuyede447/O2O_identity_audit/releases/tag/v3.2.0) and the Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.22478277 (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22478276). Raw AI-TOD-v2 and VisDrone images and annotations are not redistributed and must be obtained from their providers (https://github.com/Chasel-Tsui/AI-TOD-v2 and https://github.com/VisDrone/VisDrone-Dataset). Raw prediction dumps are not redistributed.
