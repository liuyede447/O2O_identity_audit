# Dataset access

The repository does not redistribute third-party images or annotations.

## AI-TOD-v2

- Official project repository: <https://github.com/Chasel-Tsui/AI-TOD-v2>
- Obtain and use the dataset under the terms published by its maintainers.
- Preserve the class ordering in `configs/aitod_v2.yaml`.

## VisDrone

- Official project repository: <https://github.com/VisDrone/VisDrone-Dataset>
- Obtain and use VisDrone2019-DET under the terms published by its maintainers.
- Preserve the class ordering in `configs/visdrone.yaml`.

The public files under `data/` contain derived audit values, sampling metadata,
and image identifiers only. They do not contain the original image pixels or
annotation files.
